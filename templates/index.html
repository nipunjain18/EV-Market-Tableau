Key Market Intelligence Findings

Core quantitative takeaways derived from the 100,000-model dataset.

🛵 Volume Disparity

Two-Wheeler Scooters lead global sales (85.0M units), followed by Sedans (78.3M) and Compact SUVs (76.1M). Buses and Trucks represent low volume (5.1M) but premium ticket revenue.

🔋 Battery Longevity Science

Solid-State leads with 2,999 cycles and 7.36% 5-yr degradation; LFP follows with 2,498 cycles and 10.11%. NMC, Sodium-Ion, and NCA suffer ~14% degradation.

💰 Price Drivers

Vehicle price correlates strongly with battery pack capacity (r = 0.84) rather than driving range (r = 0.28), reflecting high raw pack manufacturing expenses.

⚡ Charging Parity

Fast-charge durations average ~21.1 minutes and range averages ~324 km across all 5 chemistries, proving chemistry choice dictates lifecycle durability rather than charge speed.
