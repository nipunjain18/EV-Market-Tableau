💡 Core Analytical Insights

Key quantitative conclusions drawn from the 100,000 EV fleet analysis.

🛵 Volume Disparity

Two-Wheeler Scooters lead global sales (85.0M units), followed by Sedans (78.3M) and Compact SUVs (76.1M). Buses and Trucks sell fewer units (5.1M) but drive premium ticket revenue.

🔋 Battery Longevity Science

Solid-State leads with 2,999 cycles and 7.36% 5-yr degradation; LFP follows with 2,498 cycles and 10.11%. NMC, Sodium-Ion, and NCA degrade at ~14%.

💰 Price Regression Drivers

Retail price correlates heavily with battery pack capacity (r = 0.84) rather than driving range (r = 0.28), reflecting physical battery pack manufacturing expenses.

⚡ Charging Parity

DC fast charging averages ~21.1 minutes and range averages ~324 km across all chemistries, proving chemistry choice influences lifecycle durability rather than charge speed.
