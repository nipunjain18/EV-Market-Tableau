Project Overview & Objective

The global Electric Vehicle (EV) industry is experiencing unprecedented expansion across commercial, public transit, and personal mobility sectors. This project presents an end-to-end business intelligence lifecycle analyzing 100,000 EV models across 37 technical and commercial parameters from model years 2018 through 2026.

Technical Architecture

  1. Data Foundation: Preserved 100,000-record dataset (ev_market_test.csv) with full schema fidelity and audit of missing values.
  2. Database & SQL: Ingested into structured relational tables with custom indexes and clean analytical views (vw_ev_clean) executing 10 targeted analytical queries.
  3. Tableau Analytics: Built 12 dedicated worksheets, 3 responsive dashboards (Market Overview, Technology, Geography & Impact), and a 6-scene structured Story.
  4. Web Application: Lightweight Flask application with embedded Tableau views, responsive CSS grid, and environment-driven configurations.

Academic Integrity & Attribution
